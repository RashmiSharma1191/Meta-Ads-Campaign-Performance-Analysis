# 📊 Facebook & Instagram Ad Campaign Performance Dashboard  

## 🎯 **Business Objective**  
To analyze and track **Facebook and Instagram paid ad campaigns** for visibility into **reach, engagement, conversions, and budget utilization**.  
The report helps identify the best-performing platform, optimize ad spend, and understand audience behavior.  

---

## 📘 **Scope**  
**In Scope:** Facebook & Instagram campaigns  
**Out of Scope:** Messenger, Audience Network, organic engagement

## 📈 **Dashboard Insights**

### 🔹 **KPI Metrics**
- **Impressions:** 216K – strong reach  
- **Clicks:** 25.4K – high audience interest  
- **Shares:** 1.3K, **Comments:** 2.6K – solid organic engagement  
- **Purchases (Conversions):** 1.3K – real acquisitions  
- **Engagements:** 29K – total interactions  
- **CTR:** 11.76% – excellent (above avg. 1–2%)  
- **Engagement Rate:** 13.56% – very healthy  
- **Conversion Rate:** 5.21% – decent, can improve with landing page optimization  
- **Purchase Rate:** 0.61% – low funnel efficiency  
- **Total Budget:** 2.5M  
- **Avg Budget per Campaign:** 50.7K  

**Insight:** Ads perform strongly in visibility and engagement, but purchase efficiency needs improvement. Top-funnel (awareness) is effective; bottom-funnel (conversion) needs optimization.  

---

### 🔹 **Engagement Breakdown**
**By Gender:**  
- Female: 43% (13K)  
- Male: 22% (6K)  
- Other/Not Specified: 35% (10K)  
➡️ Females engage more — focus campaigns on female audiences.  

**By Age:**  
- Peak engagement: **20–30** age group  
➡️ Primary target: **Females aged 18–30**  

---

### 🔹 **Geographic Insights**
Top engaged countries: **US, India, Brazil, Germany, UK**  
➡️ Focus campaigns on **India & US** for scale, and **Germany/UK** for premium conversions.  

---

### 🔹 **Time-Based Trends**
- **Weekly Trend:** Engagement steady across weeks → sustained performance  
- **Hourly Trend:** Peaks in **late afternoon & evening (15–20 hrs)** → schedule ads then for better impact  
- **Calendar View:** Activity highest around **19th–21st & 25th–27th June** → promotion days drive engagement  

---

### 🔹 **Ad Type Analysis**
| Ad Type | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Purchase Rate | Conversion Rate | Engagement Rate |
|----------|--------------|--------|------|----------------|------------------|------------------|
| Carousel | 48K | 6K | 11.7% | 0.59% | 5.1% | 13.4% |
| Image | 51K | 6K | 11.7% | 0.57% | 4.9% | 13.5% |
| Stories | 72K | 8K | 11.8% | 0.65% | 5.2% | 13.6% |
| Video | 46K | 5K | 11.9% | 0.62% | 5.2% | 13.7% |

**Insight:**  
- **Video** and **Stories** ads perform best across all metrics.  
- **Image** and **Carousel** have decent reach but lower conversions.  

---

## 💡 **Final Recommendations**
1. Improve **landing pages** and retargeting to boost conversions  
2. Focus on **females aged 18–30**, especially in **India & Brazil**  
3. Allocate more budget to **Video** and **Stories** formats  
4. Schedule ads in **afternoons & evenings** for better results  
5. Optimize budget distribution toward **high-performing regions**
