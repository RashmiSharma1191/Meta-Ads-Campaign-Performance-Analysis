# 📊 Facebook & Instagram Ad Campaign Performance Dashboard  

## 🎯 **Business Objective**  
To analyze and track **Facebook and Instagram paid ad campaigns** for visibility into **reach, engagement, conversions, and budget utilization**.  
The report helps identify the best-performing platform, optimize ad spend, and understand audience behavior.  

---

## 📘 **Data, Tools & Scope**  
- Data Source: Facebook & Instagram Ad Campaign Data (Historical performance). 
- Scope: In Scope: Facebook & Instagram campaigns. Out of Scope: Messenger, Audience Network, organic engagement.

## 💡 **Strategic Insights & Recommendations**

The dashboard analysis reveals a strong top-funnel but significant bottlenecks in the final conversion, leading to the following strategic recommendations:
- Audience Focus: Females (43% engagement) and the 20–30 age group show peak response. Campaigns should be re-targeted primarily toward Females aged 18–30.
- Creative Allocation: Allocate more budget to Video and Stories formats, which consistently perform best across engagement and conversion metrics.
- Operational Timing: Schedule ads primarily in the late afternoon & evening (15–20 hrs), which are identified as peak activity times for maximum impact.
- Conversion Fix: Improve landing pages and strengthen retargeting efforts to significantly boost the low Purchase Rate (0.61%).
- Geographic Focus: Prioritize ad spend toward India & US for scale, and strategically target Germany/UK for premium conversions.

## 💡 **Final Recommendations**
1. Improve **landing pages** and retargeting to boost conversions  
2. Focus on **females aged 18–30**, especially in **India & Brazil**  
3. Allocate more budget to **Video** and **Stories** formats  
4. Schedule ads in **afternoons & evenings** for better results  
5. Optimize budget distribution toward **high-performing regions**
